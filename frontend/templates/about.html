About InvestSmart

InvestSmart is a web application built to help small and everyday investors make better decisions in the stock market. Many people lose money by following rumours and tips. Our goal is to give clear, honest information so you can decide for yourself and avoid unnecessary losses.

We bring together a company's financial health, its recent price behaviour, and a simple outlook for the coming week — all explained in plain language, with a small chart for every share so you can see the movement at a glance.
