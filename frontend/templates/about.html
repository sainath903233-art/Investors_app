About InvestSmart

InvestSmart is a web application designed to help retail and small-scale investors understand stock market information in a simple and accessible way. Instead of relying only on rumours, tips, or complex financial information, the platform brings relevant market data and analysis together in one place.

The system combines fundamental analysis, technical analysis, market information, risk awareness, and Generative AI to provide understandable insights about individual stocks. Users can explore a company's financial indicators, recent price behaviour, technical signals, short-term outlook, and historical backtesting results.

InvestSmart is intended as an educational and analytical platform. Its purpose is to help users understand investment information and potential risks rather than replace professional financial advice. Investment decisions should always be made independently after considering relevant information and individual circumstances.
