Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about using InvestSmart.

🚀

How do I start?

Click "Get Started" to create an account. After registering and logging in, you can access your dashboard.

🔎

How do I analyse a stock?

Go to your dashboard and search for a stock using its symbol, such as RELIANCE, TCS, INFY, HDFCBANK, or SBIN.

📊

What does the analysis include?

The stock analysis includes fundamental indicators, technical indicators, price charts, a short-term outlook, and key insights.

🤖

What are the AI insights?

Generative AI converts the available financial and technical information into simpler explanations and risk-aware insights.

📈

What is backtesting?

Backtesting evaluates historical technical signals by comparing them with subsequent price movements. It is provided for educational analysis and does not guarantee future performance.

⚙️

Why is market data empty?

Market information is retrieved from the FastAPI backend. Make sure the backend is running and available on the configured port, currently port 9000.

🛡️

Is this financial advice?

No. InvestSmart is an educational and analytical platform. Market data and analysis can change, and investment decisions should be made independently.
